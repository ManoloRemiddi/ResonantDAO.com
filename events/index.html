Your first meeting

Everyone starts the same way

Start with Resonant Academy (Tue, peer learning, everyone welcome) or Augmented Minds (Thu, the non-tech call) — both are open formats where just listening is a full way to attend. Then, by track:

  • Builder → ResonantOS Coding Academy (Wed) to see how the community codes with AI, then Friday Automation when you have something to show. The Tech Team Sync comes later, once you are contributing.
  • Vibe coder → AI Stack (Wed) to get your personal setup right, then Coding Academy right after — same evening, back to back.
  • Thinker → Augmented Minds (Thu), the non-tech, humanity-focused call — or Creative Lab (Wed) if you make things. No code required at either; that is the point.
  • Rather listen than talk? → any Stage event (Resonant Academy Tue, AI Stack Wed, Friday Automation Fri) — audience mode is built in. Speak whenever you are ready, including never.
  • Unsure → one Resonant Co-Op Engine (Sun) — an evening of hearing what everyone is building is the fastest way to find your pull.
  • Curious about the DAO / token side → ResonantDAO Exploration & Community Sync (Mon).
  • In Asia-Pacific / European evenings do not work for you → Bridge (Thu 13:00 CEST) — the one call scheduled for your day, connecting regions.
