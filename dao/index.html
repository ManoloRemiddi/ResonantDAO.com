What official membership unlocks

The contribution economy

Credentials

Non-transferable

DAO credentials are non-transferable. A member cannot sell or transfer their DAO identity to another wallet.

Rewards

Contribution is rewarded

Meaningful contribution across projects, communities, and individuals is recognized and rewarded inside the contribution economy.

Governance

Voting power

Voting power follows proven participation and time, not the size of a wallet.
