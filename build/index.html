Your track

Three ways of working, all real

Builder

Comfortable with code, repos, local models. First step: pick a small issue in the ResonantOS repo (look for “help wanted”) and ship an AI-assisted bugfix PR.

github.com/ResonantOS

Vibe Coder

You build with AI doing the heavy lifting. First step: have your agent write or improve a README for a ResonantOS repo — and post what you learned.

github.com/ResonantOS

Thinker

Artist, writer, philosopher, strategist — vision, not code. First step: read the manifesto, then bring one question or challenge to a community call.

Read the manifesto

Unsure? Say hi on Discord and lurk a call or two. Watching is contribution here — really.

Matchmaking

Sunday is the night projects meet people

The Resonant Co-Op Engine, Sundays: 90-second pitches, then breakout rooms. The fastest way to find collaborators — and the natural exit for the “I’d build if only…” row.

Small beats big. Choice beats assignment. Work happens in small self-organizing teams around bounties with clear scope and reward.
