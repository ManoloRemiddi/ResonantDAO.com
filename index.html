ResonantDAO

ResonantDAO is the community layer around ResonantOS.

Members join by agreeing to the Augmentatism Manifesto.

Membership is represented by non-transferable NFTs locked to one wallet.

The Community NFT becomes the access key for future DAO smart contracts.
