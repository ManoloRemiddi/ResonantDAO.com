The front door

One hello, a human answers

The most important step is not a form or a download. Post one hello in #introductions — a human will answer. That is how this community works: guides and agents come second; people come first.

Do it when you are ready

For some that is hour one. For some it is week three, after quietly watching a few calls. Both are on time.

Answering is the next step

The person who answers your first hello was a newcomer once. Answering the next hello is one of the first contributions available to you — and the one that grows this place fastest.

Ask about what pulls you

A project you are carrying, plain curiosity, the philosophy, or the people — name yours in your introduction. A named why is an anchor.

For the quiet ones

You can attend without performing

Every Discord Stage event — Resonant Academy, AI Stack, Friday Automation — has listener/speaker separation built in. You can attend purely as audience, and nobody can put you on the spot.

Camera optional

Camera off, mic off, just listening is a full way to attend anything here. Speak whenever you are ready — including never.

Watching counts

Watching calls is contribution. Using someone’s tool and telling them what broke is contribution. The audience is the market, not a holding pen.

Not everyone is made for the stage

The band is only one of the roles. So is the sound tech, the light design, the manager. Test someone’s tool. Fix a wrong meeting time. Answer a question in a channel.

The one rule

Anti-Capture

You are sovereign. Nobody prescribes what you build — and you never capture anyone else’s sovereignty. That is the Law of Anti-Capture, and it is the ethical floor for everything here, including the AI tools the community ships.
