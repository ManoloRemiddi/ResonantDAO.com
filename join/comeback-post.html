The band's getting back together 🎸

If you were part of this community and drifted away — because you couldn't see where things were going, or because nobody showed you where to plug in — that was a real gap on our side, not a failure on yours. So we fixed it.

There's now a proper front door: a Welcome Guide, a map of all 12 weekly calls, a who-does-what map, a project registry — and an Onboarding Agent you can drop into any AI and just ask questions. (Link: Welcome Guide — start at "Coming back? Read this first.")

And the progress you couldn't see is now checkable: ResonantOS shipped its Living Archive V1 and develops in the open on GitHub, the DAO design is written down, and members are building real projects — the registry lists them.

No catching-up required, no explanation owed, nobody will ask where you've been. Drop into any daily call (Mon–Fri, 7:45 PM Berlin) or just DM Frinzfrinz. Your old spot is still yours.

The stage got built while you were away. Come see it.
