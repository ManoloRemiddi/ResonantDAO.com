Meeting Map — Augmentatism Community

All recurring calls of the Augmentatism community in one place. Meetings happen on our Discord (voice channels and Stage) or on Zoom — always take the live Zoom link from the Discord event, not from bookmarks. All times are Berlin time (Europe/Berlin — currently CEST, UTC+2).

Transparency note: Meeting data collected by Frinzfrinz from the Discord events; structure, descriptions, and recommendations drafted with AI assistance (Claude). All facts trace to the Discord events, and hosts have confirmed their entries.

A note for the quiet ones: every Discord Stage event (Resonant Academy, AI Stack, Friday Automation) has listener/speaker separation built in — you can attend purely as audience, and nobody can put you on the spot. The daily Onboarding call is 15 minutes and explicitly fine to just listen in. Camera off, mic off, just listening is a full way to attend anything here.

What each meeting is (one breath each)

  1. Member Onboarding: Get Connected — 15-minute daily huddle to introduce yourself, ask anything, or just listen. Explicitly low-pressure: "no high-pressure pitches, just a casual space to sync up and get oriented."
  2. ResonantDAO Exploration & Community Sync — 90 minutes on the DAO: token economics, social dynamics, growth, governance, plus updates on what's being built (including ResonantOS) and upcoming initiatives.
  3. ResonantOS — Tech Team Sync — The dev team's weekly working call: updates, blockers, priorities, milestones. Join if you're contributing code; observe once you're oriented.
  4. Resonant Academy — Community teaching community. Peer-led learning sessions on Stage.
  5. AI Stack — "Hardware to harness": designing your personal AI stack (local LLMs, Ollama, vLLM, inference engines…) with personal advice. Come with your setup questions.
  6. Creative Lab — Bring half-finished projects and stuck pieces; get real feedback; make things together. "This isn't about tech or AI. It's about craft, expression, and the human side of making."
  7. ResonantOS Coding Academy — AI-assisted development practices and AI workflow, with live Q&A. The on-ramp for coding with AI in this community.
  8. Weekly Far East Meeting — Discussion, updates, and networking across the Far East: regional developments, business, culture, tech. The community's Asia-Pacific anchor (7:00 PM GMT+8 / 6:00 PM CST China).
  9. Augmented Minds — The weekly non-tech gathering: humanity and community. Also the recorded flagship call (see the YouTube archive).
  10. Friday Automation — The project showcase. Present what you built; reserve a slot in advance (message Mel DeWeese or ximonomix on Discord). This is the community's main stage for showing your work.
  11. Community Leader Session — Biweekly, for leaders and leader-curious: how to better support members and keep the community welcoming. Check the Discord event for the next date.
  12. The Resonant Co-Op Engine — Matchmaking sync: 5' grounding → 25' Match Floor (rapid 90-second outlines of projects, skills, proposals) → 15' cell breakouts to exchange contacts and coordinate; the event block runs to midnight for open collaboration. Exchanges are between members; the organizer only facilitates (disclaimer).

Your first meeting

Everyone starts the same way: join Member Onboarding: Get Connected (any weekday, 7:45 PM Berlin). It exists precisely so your first call is easy — 15 minutes, say hi or just listen. Both count.

Then, by track:

  • Builder → ResonantOS Coding Academy (Wed) to see how the community codes with AI, then Friday Automation when you have something to show. The Tech Team Sync comes later, once you're contributing.
  • Vibe coder → AI Stack (Wed) to get your personal setup right, then Coding Academy right after — same evening, back to back.
  • Thinker → Augmented Minds (Thu) — the non-tech, humanity-focused call — or Creative Lab (Wed) if you make things. No code required at either; that's the point.
  • Rather listen than talk? → any Stage event (Resonant Academy Tue, AI Stack Wed, Friday Automation) — audience mode is built in — plus the daily Onboarding call in listen-only mode. Speak whenever you're ready, including never.
  • Unsure → the daily Onboarding call plus one Resonant Co-Op Engine (Sun) — an evening of hearing what everyone's building is the fastest way to find your pull.
  • Curious about the DAO/token side → ResonantDAO Exploration & Community Sync (Mon).
  • In Asia-Pacific / European evenings don't work for you → Weekly Far East Meeting (Thu 1:00 PM Berlin / 7:00 PM GMT+8) — the one call scheduled for your day.

Known wrinkles (honest by design)

  • Wednesday is crowded: AI Stack (7–9 PM) and Creative Lab (8–10 PM) overlap by an hour, with Coding Academy at 10 PM. If you're torn: Creative Lab is deliberately non-tech, AI Stack is setup-tech — pick by what you need this week.
  • One Zoom room, many meetings: back-to-back calls share Manolo's Zoom room. If you land mid-meeting, you're probably early for the next one — hang out or check the Discord event.
  • Almost everything runs on European evenings. The Far East Meeting (Thu 1:00 PM Berlin) is currently the only Asia-Pacific-friendly slot — worth knowing before you recommend meetings to someone in another timezone.
  • Times drift. Trust the Discord event over this document. If you find a mismatch, tell the maintainer — that's a contribution.
