Project Registry — Augmentatism Community

A living list of what's being built in this community — the anchor projects and the member projects around them. Two purposes: help you find people building things close to yours, and surface overlaps worth a conversation. Every project here started the same way yours will: someone posted it.

This registry is also the source of truth for the building axis — the axis that is separate from participation. In the Tiers and Labels document, labels are derived from this table, never self-assigned: status, shipped cycles, external proof, and contribution to other builds. Participation (watching, learning, feedback) belongs to the warm axis and needs no record here.

It is also where friction becomes visible. A Looking for column that names a block (help to start, a teammate, a tool explained) marks a potential builder — the community's focus. Those rows are reviewed at the daily onboarding call, and the reply each one gets is one next action, not a syllabus. See Tiers and Labels for the rules.

Add yourself: answer Q5 of the Expertise Questionnaire when you introduce yourself, or post in the Showcase your project thread on our Discord — the maintainer picks new entries up from both.

Coming back after time away? Your old project has a place here — reclaim your row, update it, or mark it paused. Paused is an honest status, and a paused project someone else can learn from beats a vanished one.

Entries are seeded from public showcase posts on our Discord. Owners: your row is yours — where a field shows "—", fill it in; where something's wrong, say so. This document is only as good as its last update, and correcting it is a contribution.

Possible synergies

Overlaps spotted while seeding. These are invitations to talk, not merge orders — per the manifesto, "what can your AI do that mine can't?" beats consolidation.

  1. Local AI on constrained hardware — Trilobyte (Jetson Orin Nano), Kyle_Vain (Pi4/Wyse), and Frinzfrinz (consumer gaming PC) are running the same experiment at three price points. A shared benchmark or findings thread would save all three from rediscovering each other's dead ends. The AI-stack island on the Who-Does-What Map is the natural home.
  2. Personal idea-capture assistants — several members are building personal idea-capture tools independently. Same pattern, same opportunity: compare architectures before building the same thing four times. If that's you, add your row above — that's how this section fills in.
  3. Transcription ↔ Living Archive — Open-transcribe's audio/video analysis and ResonantOS's Living Archive V1 sit next to each other. Worth one conversation about whether one can feed the other (e.g. community call recordings → searchable archive).
  4. Agent orchestration — Frinzfrinz's agent start-up and ResonantOS's Hermes/OpenCode delegation are both wiring up local agent teams. Findings likely transfer in both directions.

What's not here yet

This registry is a snapshot of the public showcase thread — projects living in other channels, or in nobody's posts yet, are missing by definition. That's not a flaw; it's the invitation. Add yours via the questionnaire (Q5) or the showcase thread, and it appears here.
