Who-Does-What Map

This is not an org chart. There is no hierarchy here — the community's one rule is sovereignty, and Manolo's own framing is "I show how it's done, I don't tell you how." Think of this map as islands, not a tree: areas of the community, and the people who tend them. It answers one question: who do I ask about X?

Discord roles you'll see in the member list (Custodian, R-Moderator, Community Leader, Pioneer-Tech) describe involvement, not rank.

All reach paths go through our Discord unless noted otherwise.
