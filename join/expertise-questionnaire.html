Expertise Questionnaire — Tell Us About You

A short set of questions for newcomers to the Augmentatism community. Your answers are posted publicly in the #introductions channel on our Discord — they double as your introduction, so other members can reply, point you somewhere useful, or say hello. Nothing here is collected privately or used for anything you can't see happening in the thread. (That's not fine print — it's the Law of Anti-Capture applied to our own onboarding.)

Answer as much or as little as you like. "Unsure" and "I'm just here to watch for now" are complete, valid answers — watching is a level on the evolution ladder, not a failure state.

One thing worth knowing before you answer: this questionnaire never labels you. It tells the community how you want to work and where you are right now. Labels (Audience, Potential Builder, Tinkerer, Shipper, Operator) are a different thing: they describe what you have shipped and what you have named as the next step, and they are derived from the Project Registry, never from a form. You will not be graded on these answers, ever.

Takes about 5 minutes.

Copy everything between the lines, replace the placeholders, and post it in the #introductions channel on our Discord.

What happens next: real people reply — that's the point. Expect a human hello, a pointer to your first meeting (see the Meeting Map), and if you named a project in Q5, an invitation to add it to the Project Registry — an idea with a friction named is a complete row, and it is exactly the kind of row the community focuses on. Questions about local AI setups and hardware find their home in the AI-stack channel on our Discord.
