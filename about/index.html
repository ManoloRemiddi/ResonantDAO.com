About ResonantDAO

A DAO for the Resonant Economy.

ResonantDAO is designed as a Community of Communities: a human-first economic layer for the age of AI, built to recognize contribution, coordinate governance, and support people as AI changes how value is created.

Human-first economy

The DAO exists to build an economy with AI that sustains humans, rather than reducing human value to attention metrics or capital.

Membership is not a market

ResonantDAO credentials are non-transferable. A member cannot sell or transfer their DAO identity to another wallet.

Contribution unlocks power

Governance is designed around contribution and time, not token quantity, so the economy cannot be captured by capital alone.

Community unlocks access

The Community NFT is the credential future Resonant Economy smart contracts can check before allowing access to member-only actions.
