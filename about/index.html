About ResonantDAO

A DAO for people building around ResonantOS.

ResonantDAO is designed as a Community of Communities: a simple, human-centered coordination layer for people who want to participate in the evolution of ResonantOS.

Membership is not a market

ResonantDAO credentials are non-transferable. A member cannot sell or transfer their DAO identity to another wallet.

Agreement comes first

The first step is to sign the Augmentatism Manifesto. That agreement is recorded as a proof NFT before Community membership is issued.

Community unlocks access

The Community NFT is the credential future DAO smart contracts can check before allowing access to member-only actions.

ResonantOS remains connected

The Symbiotic Licence and Pioneer credentials are for Community members who participate early in ResonantOS co-ownership and growth.
